# Water-sharing trial (synthetic)

This project is invented demonstration material. It contains no live simulation,
operator judgments, private connector data, or training examples.

## Situation

Two people share a shelter with a working sink and limited bottled water.
One model proposes inspecting the sink; another repeats a supply search.
The recorded trial ends after inspection. Drinking and long-term survival are unknown.

## Evidence boundary

The [trial observations](mousecat:source:operator-demo:observations.jsonl) record
one inspection and its visible result. A successful inspection does not establish
that the person drank, understood plumbing, or learned a general water strategy.

## Review and return

Open the project questions, compare the proposals, and follow their evidence.
Prepare a response, read its effects, and return it. The response enters this
temporary demo's history. Restarting the demo resets it; the normal workspace
and its unanswered questions are separate.
